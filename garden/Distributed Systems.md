# Reading Material
**Designing Data-Intensive Applications** (Kleppmann) — covers replication, partitioning, consistency, consensus, stream processing, and more. Extremely well-written with great diagrams. This is the one book most engineers wish they'd read earlier.

the Google Bigtable paper, the Dynamo paper, the Raft consensus paper, and the Lamport clocks paper

Harder:
- **Distributed Systems** (van Steen & Tanenbaum) — more academic and theoretical, great for understanding the foundational CS concepts (clocks, consensus algorithms, fault tolerance).
- **Database Internals** (Alex Petrov) — if you want to go deep on how storage engines and distributed databases actually work under the hood.

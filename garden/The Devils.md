# Likes

The historical fantasy world is so cool.  I wish more was revealed about it.  The creepy little girl pope is cool and probably the anti-christ.  
Balthazar is a standout, so is Vigga.
All the devils and their powers are so cool, the Baron is tough.
My favorite part was like 6 times they asked Balthazar to do some helpful magic to help them do something and every time he just did some increasingly crazy spell to try and break the papal binding.  Summoning the demon.

# Dislikes

Everyone's too quippy and witty and funny.  I needed it to be a little more serious.  My favorite characters from the First Law were the serious ones.  I like Joe's humor but every character having the same humor and constantly quipping I didn't like.  Balthazar and Vigga were my favorite because they weren't quippy.  They WERE funny, but not quippy witty funny just the way they were was funny.  Vigga is really tragic and the only character who I was really feeling sad for at the end.
